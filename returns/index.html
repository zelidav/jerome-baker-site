Glass returns

You can return glass within 14 days of delivery as long as it is unused and in its original packaging and condition.

  • Email info@jeromebakerny.com with your order number before you send anything back. We will reply with the return address and instructions.
  • Pack it the way it came. Glass that arrives back broken because it was packed badly cannot be refunded.
  • Return shipping is on you, unless we sent the wrong piece.
  • Once the piece is back and checked, we refund the price of the piece to your original payment method. Original shipping is not refunded.

Broken in shipping

If glass arrives broken, we replace it.

  • Email info@jeromebakerny.com within 7 days of delivery with your order number.
  • Photos may be required: the piece, the box and the packing, before you throw anything out. Keep the packaging until we have sorted it out, in case the carrier needs to see it.
  • Most of our glass is one of a kind. If the exact piece cannot be made again, we will work with you on a comparable piece or refund it.

Merch

Ritual Merch is printed to order, so we cannot take merch back for size, color or a change of mind. Check sizes before you order.

If merch arrives with a manufacturing error (wrong item, misprint, flaw) or damaged in shipping, email info@jeromebakerny.com within 14 days of delivery with your order number and a photo, and we will give you store credit for it.

Not returnable

  • Glass that has been used, or is not in its original packaging and condition.
  • Merch, other than store credit for a manufacturing error or breakage as above.

Warranty

Our promise is the one on this page: we stand behind the piece arriving as described and in one piece. Damage after delivery, like drops, knocks or heat and cold shock, is not covered. See our terms for the full warranty disclaimer.

Questions

Anything not covered here, or a problem with an order: info@jeromebakerny.com. Include your order number and we will sort it out.
