Where Lookie Lou comes from

A Jerome Baker booth wall

This is one of our booth walls. We've been hauling walls like this to shows for years. Hand sketches, murals, posters and stickers, all ours, all on barn wood. Lookie Lou started out right here. JB fam, if you see us out and about, come find Lookie Lou on the wall.
