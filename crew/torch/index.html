The JB Crew · No. 01

Torch

The lion on the torch

Torch has had the flame up and the hat on since the first piece. He blows glass on a skateboard because standing still is for vases. Hood stays down. Fire code.

Hold still. I'm pulling a bubble.

Who they are

The perfectionist with a short fuse

From Eugene, Oregon

The backstory

Learned the torch in a garage in 1991 from a guy who never wrote anything down. Survived the raid, the barn years and three landlords. Opened The Hot Shop because nobody else would hire the crew, and he regrets it daily.

At the Hot Shop: Gaffer and owner. Runs the bench, signs every piece.

Vibes with

Craftspeople, perfectionists, OG heads who remember 1999, anyone who's been doing one thing for thirty years

Loves

A clean gather, the smell of fumed silver, people who show up on time

Hates
