Torch

The lion on the torch

Torch has been in the little window on the booth wall since the first show, flame up, hat on. He blows glass on a skateboard because standing still is for vases. Hood stays down. Fire code.

Hold still. I'm pulling a bubble.

Who they are

The perfectionist with a short fuse

From Eugene, Oregon

The backstory

Learned the torch in a garage in 1991 from a guy who never wrote anything down. Has been in the little window on the booth wall since the first show. Survived the raid, the barn years and three landlords. Opened The Hot Shop because nobody else would hire the crew, and he regrets it daily.

At the Hot Shop: Gaffer and owner. Runs the bench, signs every piece.
