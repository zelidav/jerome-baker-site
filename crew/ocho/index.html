Ocho

Eight arms, four flip-flops

Ocho has eight arms, hence the name, and owns four flip-flops, which he says is the correct ratio. Mostly dances. Occasionally shouts the poster at strangers. The strangers usually feel better.

ENLIGHTEN ALL HUMANS.

Who they are

The backstory

Washed up on a beach during a festival in the 90s and decided the crowd was his family. Started the poster as a joke and then meant it. Has eight arms, four flip-flops and one very strong opinion about the moon.

At the Hot Shop: Kiln master and night shift. Talks to the fire, the fire talks back.
