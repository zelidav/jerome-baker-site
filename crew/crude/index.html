Where Crude comes from

The long Jerome Baker booth wall

This is the long booth wall, the big one that goes up behind us at shows. Years of hand sketches, posters and murals, all ours, pinned to barn wood. Crude started out right here. JB fam, if you see us out and about, come find Crude on the wall.

Find Crude: The Oil Crisis poster, framed in the middle. Robots Need Oil.

Who they are

The laid-back surfer who's always half lit

From Port Arthur, Texas

The backstory

Worked the refineries on the Gulf until he realized he could just live in the oil. Came off the Oil Crisis poster and taught himself to surf on it. Has been fired from every job he's ever had, mostly for drinking the product.

At the Hot Shop: Assistant. Lubes the machines, mops the floor, mostly drinks the 30-weight.
