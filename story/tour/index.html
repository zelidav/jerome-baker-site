About these screenshots

Every frame is a screenshot of a capture the Internet Archive saved of jeromebaker.com, shown in the order it was saved. We looked at 835 and kept the 34 that tell the story. The 2013 to 2016 captures show someone else’s page, so they are left out. The 2019 to 2022 pages came back without their stylesheets, so they look plainer than they were.

Where the archive never saved a picture, the page still asks for one, so the frame shows an empty box. We filled those boxes with the closest thing the archive does hold (a banner from the next year, a tube photo from the same era) or a plain labelled tile, and outlined each one in dashed yellow. Nothing marked as a stand-in is the original picture. The As saved switch above any frame shows it untouched.
