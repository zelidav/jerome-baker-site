  1. 1989A parking lot outside a Dead show. A pipe that changed color the more it got used. A kid who had to know how.
  2. 1991Eugene, Oregon. A garage, a torch, and the guy who invented fuming down the street. Jerome Baker starts here.
  3. 1995Jerome Baker Designs goes official. The Mothership takes off: orb base, long neck, frogs and dragonflies at the joint.
  4. 1999Seventy glassblowers on two shifts. The biggest smokeware shop on the planet, and five Cannabis Cups on the shelf.
  5. Feb 24, 20036 a.m. The feds come through every door at once. Operation Pipe Dreams. The website gets seized. The torches go out the back to a barn.
  6. AfterTeaching. Blowing glass. Keeping the flame lit in somebody else's studio.
  7. 2026New York. Same torch, same weird. The crew came too.
