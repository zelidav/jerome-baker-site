Who we are

This site, jeromebaker.cannacrypted.com, is run by Jerome Baker Designs together with Green Garden NY LLC ("Jerome Baker", "we", "us"). Questions about this policy go to info@jeromebakerny.com.

What we collect

We only collect what you choose to send us through a form on this site:

  • Join the JB fam: first name, email, phone (if you give it), state, ZIP, what you are interested in, and whether you said yes to texts.
  • Contact: your name, email, phone (if you give it), the topic and your message.
  • Wholesale: your business name and type, contact name, email, phone, state, license number and message.
  • Feedback button: your note plus the page you were on, the part of the page in view, your screen size, browser and device type, so we can find and fix what you saw. Your name is optional.

We do not run advertising trackers or analytics scripts on this site, and we do not use cookies to follow you around the web.

How we use it

  • To answer your message, quote a custom piece or set up a wholesale account.
  • To send the drop alerts, emails and texts you signed up for.
  • To fix the site when you report something.
  • To keep records we are required to keep, and to prevent fraud and abuse.

Texts

We only text you if you ticked the box to get texts. Expect a few a month at most: drop reminders with a link. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to stop at any time, or HELP for help. Your phone number and text consent are never sold or shared with anyone for their own marketing.

Who we share it with

We do not sell or rent your information. We share it only with the companies that run things for us, and only so they can do that job:

  • Our hosting and database provider, where the forms are stored.
  • The services that send our emails and texts.
  • The customer list we use to keep track of retailers and fans.
  • Payment processors and shipping carriers, when you buy something (see below).

We will also share information if the law requires it, or to protect our rights, our customers or the public.

When you buy

Glass is bought through our studio checkout and merch through our Ritual Merch store at jeromebakerny.com. Those pages collect your shipping address and payment. Card payments are handled by the payment processor; we do not store your full card number. Our order records keep what we need to ship, support and account for the order.

Third-party content

The site loads its fonts from Google Fonts, which means Google sees the request from your browser. The story video only loads from YouTube when you press play. Links to Instagram and other sites go to services with their own privacy policies.

Settings saved on your device

A few choices, like Lite mode or hiding the crew, are saved in your own browser so the site remembers them next time. They never leave your device, and clearing your browser data removes them.

Adults only

This site is for adults 21 and over. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 21. If you think someone under 21 has sent us their details, tell us and we will delete them.

Your choices

Email info@jeromebakerny.com to see what we have about you, correct it, or have it deleted. Unsubscribe from emails with the link in any email, and from texts by replying STOP. We keep information only as long as we need it for the reasons above or as the law requires.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update it here and change the date at the top.
